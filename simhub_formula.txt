SimHub Custom Serial Device — NCalc Formula
============================================

Paste into: SimHub > Custom Serial Devices > [device] > Update messages > New message

format([DataCorePlugin.GameData.NewData.SpeedKmh],'0') + ';' + isnull([DataCorePlugin.GameData.NewData.Gear],'N') + ';' + format([DataCorePlugin.GameData.NewData.Rpms],'0') + ';' + format([DataCorePlugin.GameData.NewData.MaxRpm],'0') + ';' + format([CarSettings_RPMRedLineSetting],'0')

Update frequency: "When changes occur" (or 30-60 Hz fixed)

Output example: 120;3;7500;9000;95
  Field 1: Speed (km/h)
  Field 2: Gear (N, R, 1-8)
  Field 3: Current RPM
  Field 4: Max RPM
  Field 5: Redline as % of max RPM
